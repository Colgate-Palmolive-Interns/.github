# Procurement Data Analysis – Automation & Development

This organization hosts the **automation and software development** projects built within the **Google Workspace environment** by the Data Analysis Procurement department, **Colgate-Palmolive (Mission Hills)**.

---

## About the Area

Procurement is responsible for **purchasing the materials and services required to keep the plant's production running**, managing supplier relationships, and leading cost-saving (savings) projects across the business.

Within Procurement, us, as interns, **we support the area through quotation management, process automation, and software development projects**. Our role spans **data analysis, automation, and the design of internal tools that streamline Procurement's day-to-day operations**, leveraging Google Workspace applications (Sheets, Apps Script, Drive, Forms) to reduce manual work and improve data accuracy and consistency.
